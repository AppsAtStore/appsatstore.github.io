Introduction

Welcome to CatSay – AI Cat Translator ("we," "our," or "us"). CatSay – AI Cat Translator & Breed Identifier is a mobile application for Android that uses artificial intelligence to help cat owners better understand their cats through two core features: Voice Translation (analysing cat sounds to interpret mood) and Breed Identification (identifying a cat's breed from a photograph).

Important Disclaimer: The AI-powered features (voice translation and breed identification) are provided for entertainment and general informational purposes only. The results are generated using machine learning models and may not be 100% accurate. This app does not provide veterinary diagnoses, medical advice, or professional behavioral analysis. We strongly recommend consulting a qualified veterinarian for any health or behavioral concerns regarding your cat.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and what rights you have. By downloading or using CatSay – AI Cat Translator & Breed Identifier, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the application.

This policy complies with the Google Play Store Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other applicable privacy laws.

Information We Collect

CatSay – AI Cat Translator & Breed Identifier is designed with a privacy-first, on-device processing approach. Most data never leaves your device.

Data Type Purpose Stored? Uploaded?
Audio recordings (cat sounds) Processed locally by AI model to detect cat mood Temporarily in device cache; deleted immediately after analysis ❌ Never
Photos / Camera images Processed locally by AI model to identify cat breed Not stored by the app; only held in memory during analysis ❌ Never
Device identifiers (Advertising ID) Used by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements Managed by Google SDK ✅ By Google AdMob only
App usage data (crash reports, diagnostics) App stability and performance monitoring via Google Play Services Managed by Google Play Services ✅ By Google Play Services only
Approximate location May be inferred by AdMob for ad targeting (country/region level only) Not stored by us ✅ By Google AdMob only
Key point: We do not operate any servers that receive your audio recordings, photos, or personal information. All AI inference runs entirely on your device using TensorFlow Lite models stored within the app.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing app features: Audio recordings are analysed on-device to classify cat moods. Photos are analysed on-device to identify cat breeds.
  • Displaying advertisements: Google AdMob uses device identifiers to show relevant ads. This helps keep the app free for users.
  • App stability: Crash reports collected by Google Play Services help us identify and fix bugs.
  • Improving user experience: We may analyse aggregated, anonymised usage patterns to improve the app.

We do not use your data for profiling, selling to third parties, or any purpose beyond those listed above.
